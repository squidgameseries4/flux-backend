Privacy Policy — Flox

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Flox is a social video app (videos, Shorts, posts, chat spaces). This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account information: your username, handle and password. Passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes (bcrypt) — we cannot see your plain password.
  • Profile information: display name, bio/description, profile photo and channel banner you upload.
  • Content you create: videos, Shorts, thumbnails, posts, comments, playlists and Space messages/attachments you upload.
  • Activity: likes, subscriptions and content metadata needed to run feeds, search and notifications.
  • On-device data: watch history, app preferences (e.g. theme) and login session are stored locally on your device and are not sent anywhere except the Flox server features described below.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the app: showing feeds and profiles, playing your uploads, delivering notifications, and keeping you signed in. We do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Where it is stored

Account and content data is stored on the Flox server infrastructure (hosted database and object storage). Uploaded videos and images are served from the same infrastructure. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Sharing

Content you mark Public (videos, Shorts, posts, your public channel page) is visible to other users of the service — that is the point of the app. We do not share private account data with third parties except the hosting providers that run the servers, who process it only to provide the service.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile, delete your own videos, Shorts, posts and comments inside the app.
  • You can sign out at any time from the account menu.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact the app operator through the support channel listed in the Play Store listing.

Children

Flox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the support details in the Google Play listing.
